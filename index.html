Schools & Students

Applied AI, in your classroom

A small, low-risk pilot where students use AI to work on Sustainable Development Goals they already study.

Why now

Schools are already moving this way

NEP 2020 and CBSE's evolving skill frameworks are pushing AI literacy earlier into school life. The question is how to do it well.

  • Students who apply AI to real problems retain more than those who only study it in theory.
  • A hands-on pilot lets the school evaluate the idea with minimal risk before any wider commitment.
What students do

Real problems, not abstract theory

Energy

Usage patterns and conservation ideas.

Water

Consumption and waste trends.

Climate & air

Reading local environmental data.

How it works

Built on real industry tools

1. Explore with Python

Students work with real datasets in the language used in professional data science.

2. Insights with Claude & generative AI

They summarize patterns, brainstorm solutions, and explain findings in plain language.

3. Propose solutions

Each student turns their analysis into a practical sustainability idea to present.

The pilot

A small, low-risk starting point

Grade / section

One section, Grade 9 or 10

Duration

8 weekly sessions, 45–60 minutes

Format

Hands-on and project-based; no coding background needed

Facilitation

Co-led with an existing CS or EVS teacher

For the school

What this means for you

  • Differentiation: a tangible answer to "what is the school doing on AI?" for parents and the board.
  • Zero cost, zero disruption: no budget or curriculum change needed to start, and it is co-facilitated with your own teacher.
  • A showcase moment: final-session student presentations make a ready-made feature for a newsletter or parent event.
